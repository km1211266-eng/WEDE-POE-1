An honest cup, roasted close to home

We started in a spare room with a one-kilogram sample roaster and a stubborn refusal to buy pre-roasted green. Two years on we still roast in batches small enough to taste every one of them, supply a handful of Johannesburg cafés, and ship bags out on the same week they come off the drum.

Poured from the drum

How a bag gets to you

1

Cup the lot

Every new arrival is cupped blind against the last harvest before we commit to a bag.

2

Rest the green

Beans sit for a minimum of three weeks so moisture levels settle before roasting.

3

Roast in small batches

Six kilograms at a time on our refurbished drum roaster, profiled by ear as much as by graph.

4

Rest again

Roasted beans degas for 24 to 96 hours depending on the roast, so you never get a bag that's too fresh to brew well.

5

Bag and date it

Every bag is stamped with its roast date, not a "best before" that hides how old it really is.
